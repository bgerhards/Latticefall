00Answer

Where combat effects come from

We synthesize 42 of the 60 from code, and source the remaining 18 as CC0 recordings that get layered with synthesis.

Everything on this page was generated by tools/audio/synth_sfx.py in 1.7 seconds. No downloads, no sample library, no licence to track. Each sound is a pure function of its name, so the bank rebuilds byte-identical anywhere, and a sound is a diff — if the pulse turret is too bright, that's a number in a file, not a re-download and a re-edit.

Press play. This is the real output, not a mockup.

Why not Suno → Suno writes music: bars, structure, an arrangement. A 40 ms interface tick or a turret discharge has none of those. Asking it for combat effects gets you short songs, not sounds.
01Listen

The bank — 35 sounds

Waveforms are real peak envelopes drawn from the samples. Crest is peak-to-RMS ratio — it's the number that tells you whether a sound has punch or has been crushed flat. Loops are marked; they're built at durations where every partial completes a whole number of cycles, so they repeat without a seam.

Listen for → Confirm and Deny are an open fifth and a minor second. That's the score bible's interval language applied to interface audio, so the UI and the soundtrack argue from the same harmonic vocabulary instead of coexisting by accident.
02The sixty

Full bank plan

Synthesis wins wherever a sound is electrical, energetic, or interface — which is most of this game. It loses on organic complexity: footsteps on stone, debris, weather, anything with a voice. Those get sourced.

GroupCountMethodContents
Sourcing rule → Freesound filtered to CC0 only. Not CC-BY — attribution obligations across dozens of clips is a liability nobody maintains, and one missed credit is a licence breach. Every sourced clip is logged in assets/audio/SOURCES.md with URL, author, licence and SHA-256 before it enters the repo.
Alternative if you'd rather pay than curate → ElevenLabs Sound Effects is text-to-SFX with commercial rights on paid tiers, and would cover those 18 organic ones the same way Suno covers the score. I'd still layer its output with synthesis. Your call; CC0 is the default I'll proceed with.
03Pipeline

Regenerating

.venv/bin/python tools/audio/synth_sfx.py # rebuild the bank
.venv/bin/python tools/audio/synth_sfx.py ui_click # one sound
.venv/bin/python tools/audio/synth_sfx.py --list # what exists
.venv/bin/python tools/audio/make_soundboard.py # rebuild this page

WAVs land in assets/audio/sfx/ at 44.1 kHz mono. Godot import settings and the Ogg encode happen at build time, not here — the repo keeps the lossless masters.

Blocked → ffmpeg on your machine is broken: Library not loaded: libx265.215.dylib. Not needed for these, but the music loop pipeline needs it. Run brew reinstall ffmpeg when convenient.
