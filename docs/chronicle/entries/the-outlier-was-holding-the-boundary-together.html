anchor-17 is not outside Act III's band — it is the last point before the plateau, and the only thing making an act boundary continuous

BALdecision 095

Decision 095, and the smallest thing on the board today: a refusal with three measurements and nothing changed. LF-274 said anchor-17's leak budget was mis-authored because it sits at 16.1% where Act III's other seven sit in a 20.7–21.3% band. Plotting all twenty-four showed the band is a description of anchors 18–24 and of nothing else in the game, and that the step into anchor-17 is +0.000 — an act boundary that is continuous, which raising it to 24 lives would break.

Twenty-six minutes after it-does-not-fail-it-lies, and the last thing of the day. LF-274 was filed by decision 093's slice and is refused here. No data file moved, no instrument was written, no screenshot was taken — the whole change is a backlog line closing and a decision entry. It is in the journal because the measurement is worth more than the item was.

The claim. anchor-17 spends 16.1% of its wave table's total leak_cost on lives — 18 of 112 — where Act III's other seven anchors sit in a 20.7–21.3% band, tight enough to look authored to. That band puts anchor-17 at 23 or 24 lives. It read as an obvious mis-authoring that had simply been held back so a grade delta could be attributed later.

Plot the other twenty-three

lives over the wave table's total leak_cost, every anchor in the campaign, computed from data/anchors/ and data/enemies.json under decision 047's max(1, round(hp/130)). Nobody had ever drawn it.

  • No integer satisfies the band. 0.207–0.213 of 112 leak_cost is lives in [23.18, 23.86]. 23 lives is 0.2054, below the band; 24 is 0.2143, above it. The rule the finding proposes has no solution, which is the first sign it is not the rule.
  • Acts I and II have no band at all. Spreads: act I 0.042 (0.083–0.125), act II 0.073 (0.087–0.160), act III excluding anchor-17 0.006. Only Act III's seven are flat. The other sixteen are ragged, with ordinary swings of 0.03 to 0.07 — anchor-14 +0.069, anchor-15 −0.039, anchor-16 +0.042. So "sit inside your act's band" is not a campaign rule; it is a description of anchors 18–24 and of nothing else in the game.
  • The decisive one: anchor-16 to anchor-17 is +0.000. 0.1603 to 0.1607, a step of four ten-thousandths, across an act boundary — against −0.032 at act I to act II. anchor-17 is not an outlier from Act III. It is the last point of the ragged region that runs from anchor-01, and the plateau begins one anchor later at anchor-18, which steps +0.052.

Change nothing. Raising anchor-17 to 24 lives turns the campaign's smoothest act boundary into one of its largest steps — +0.054 at the boundary, second only to anchor-14's +0.069 — in order to match a plateau that starts after it. That is fitting an anchor to a pattern it is not part of, the same move decision 067 rejected when it deleted a pressure floor and decision 093 rejected an hour earlier when it declined to rebase a density floor. anchor-17 also grades ok today, so nothing is being tolerated either.

What the plot found is worth more than the item: the campaign's leak budget is ragged by construction for sixteen anchors and flat for the last seven. Whether Act III should be the only act with a flat leak budget is a real design question, it is now visible, and it is deliberately not answered here — answering it means moving up to seven anchors' lives and re-grading, which belongs with BAL-04's capacity-ladder slice where a grade delta can be attributed to something.

One correction, recorded rather than patched

Decision 095 and the commit body both call anchor-16 to anchor-17 the smoothest transition in the entire campaign. Recomputed for this entry, it is the smoothest act boundary but the third-smallest step overall: anchor-18 to anchor-19 is exactly zero (both are 26 lives over 122 leak_cost) and anchor-10 to anchor-11 is −0.0003, both of them mid-act. The refusal is untouched by this — the argument only ever needed the boundary to be continuous, and it is. The same recomputation puts the cost of raising anchor-17 at +0.054 where the decision rounds it to +0.053. Both stand as written in docs/DECISIONS.md, because that file and this journal are append-only; this paragraph is the correction.

The lesson, and it is the third refusal of this shape in one session. An anchor that sits outside a band is evidence about the band as much as about the anchor. Before moving the one value that disagrees, plot the other twenty-three — here the "outlier" was the only point making an act boundary continuous.
