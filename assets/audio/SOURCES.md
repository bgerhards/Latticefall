# Sourced audio manifest

Every audio file in this repo that was not generated by this repo's own tools is logged
here **before** it is committed, with the right under which it is used.

A repository that cannot say why it is allowed to contain a file is one bad question away
from having to delete it. Decision 038.

---

## Sound effects — CC0 only

Rule: **CC0 only.** Not CC-BY, not CC-BY-SA, not a site's bespoke "free for commercial use"
licence, and not a bare public-domain mark. CC0 is an explicit, worldwide, irrevocable
waiver by the author; the others each carry either an obligation or a jurisdiction
argument. Decisions 009 and 038.

Sourcing is filtered on **machine-readable licence metadata**, never on a page that says
"free". `tools/audio/fetch_cc0.py` refuses anything whose licence field is not CC0.

## Music — generated by the project owner

The fourteen tracks in `assets/audio/music/` were generated by the project owner using a
**paid Suno subscription**, and the owner holds the rights to ship them in this game. They
are not CC0 and are not third-party works; the CC0 rule above governs sourced sound
effects and does not apply to them.

Masters are not in git (decision 012). They live in `~/Latticefall-masters/` and are
verified by the SHA-256 recorded per track in `assets/audio/music_manifest.json`, which is
the integrity record; this file is the provenance record.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/audio/music/*.ogg` | 14 | Generated with Suno, paid subscription, by the project owner | Owner's own generated content under their subscription | `assets/audio/music_manifest.json` |

## Synthesized sound effects — no third-party rights

Not listed individually. The 35 cues in `assets/audio/sfx/` are generated by
`tools/audio/synth_sfx.py`, each a pure function of its own name, and carry no third-party
rights at all. `tools/check.py` asserts they rebuild byte-identical.
